Slide 1 — Introduction: What is Marketing Strategy?

- Marketing strategy is a plan used by a business to attract customers and achieve its marketing goals.
- It identifies who the customers are, what they need, and how the business can satisfy those needs.
- It helps a company decide what to offer, at what price, where to sell, and how to promote it.

Key idea:

«The right product → to the right customer → through the right strategy.»

---

Slide 2 — Understanding the Market

1. Market Research

- Study customer needs, preferences, and behaviour.
- Identify market trends and opportunities.
- Analyse competitors.

2. Target Market

- Divide the market into different customer groups.
- Select the group the business wants to serve.

Example:
A sportswear brand may target young people interested in fitness and active lifestyles.

---

Slide 3 — The Marketing Mix (4Ps)

A major part of marketing strategy is the 4Ps:

- Product — What the business offers to customers.
- Price — How much customers pay.
- Place — Where and how the product is distributed.
- Promotion — How the business communicates with customers.

Example:
A smartphone company may offer a feature-rich phone, competitive pricing, online distribution, and social-media advertising.

---

Slide 4 — Promotion & Customer Engagement

Businesses use different methods to reach and retain customers:

- Advertising — TV, print, websites, social media.
- Sales promotion — Discounts, offers, and coupons.
- Public relations — Building a positive brand image.
- Digital marketing — Social media, email, websites, and content.
- Customer relationships — Feedback, loyalty programmes, and personalised communication.

The goal is not only to attract customers, but also to build long-term relationships.

---

Slide 5 — Conclusion

- A marketing strategy provides a clear direction for a business.
- Understanding the market and target customers is essential.
- The 4Ps help businesses develop and deliver the right marketing offer.
- Effective promotion and customer engagement can create brand awareness, customer loyalty, and business growth.

Final Thought:

«A successful marketing strategy connects what a business offers with what customers actually need.»
